Plaza PR is a boutique communications consultancy for luxury hospitality brands with stories worth telling.

Founded by hotel publicist Krisha Kotak, Plaza PR brings over ten years of experience with some of the world's most prestigious hotel brands, including Bvlgari, Rosewood and St. Regis.

Based between London, Marbella and Miami, the consultancy has cultivated a global network of editors, creators and industry tastemakers, delivering creative campaigns that connect brands with the audiences that matter most.
