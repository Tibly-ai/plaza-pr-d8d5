How much does a retainer for a luxury hospitality PR agency cost?

Retainer pricing at Plaza PR depends on scope, not on a fixed package tier — the site currently lists six ways of working (strategic PR campaigns, media relations, brand partnerships, influencer collaborations, copywriting & storytelling, and identity & positioning), and a retainer can combine as many or as few of these as a property needs. Because every client works with founder Krisha Kotak directly rather than a junior team, retainers are priced per relationship rather than per seat.

What's the average cost of hiring a boutique PR firm for a product launch?

For a single moment — a suite unveiling, restaurant opening, or seasonal launch — Plaza PR scopes a project fee rather than a retainer.   • Scope of services — a single service (e.g. media relations only) costs less than a combined retainer across all six offerings listed on the Services page.
  • Number of markets — London, Marbella and Miami each draw on a distinct part of the network; covering all three costs more than one city.
