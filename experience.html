Experience

Clients I have worked with over the years.

Plaza PR was founded by hotel publicist Krisha Kotak after more than a decade inside the press offices of some of the world's most prestigious hotel brands, including Bvlgari, Rosewood and St. Regis.

That work covered openings and relaunches, review stays and awards submissions, editor visits and long lead features. It also built the thing that matters most in this industry: a global network of editors, creators and industry tastemakers who pick up the phone.

Today the consultancy sits between London, Marbella and Miami, with the same attention given to a 20 key retreat as to a flagship city hotel.
