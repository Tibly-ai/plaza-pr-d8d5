The founder

Krisha Kotak, hotel publicist

Krisha founded Plaza PR after more than ten years representing some of the most prestigious hotel brands in the world, including Bvlgari, Rosewood and St. Regis. Those years were spent inside the detail of hospitality communications: openings, restaurant launches, seasonal moments, review stays, awards submissions and the long relationships that make all of it possible.

The consultancy model came from a simple observation. Luxury brands are rarely short of beautiful assets. What they need is someone who can find the line that makes an editor stop, and who knows which editor to send it to. Plaza PR is built around that judgement rather than around volume.

Every client works with Krisha directly, from the first strategy conversation through to the coverage report.

The network

“Coverage follows relationships. Ten years of them, with the editors, creators and tastemakers who shape where people choose to stay.”

Plaza PR has cultivated a global network across luxury travel and lifestyle media, and delivers creative campaigns that connect brands with the audiences that matter most to them. Some of that work is a magazine feature. Some of it is a review stay, an awards entry, a partnership with a wellness brand, or a creator whose images do more for a property than any press release could.
