What I can

Six ways of working, used on their own or together. Some brands come to me for a single launch moment, others for a year of steady coverage and partnerships. Every engagement starts with the story you already have.

London · Marbella · Miami

  1. 01

    Strategic PR campaigns

    Press campaigns built around what makes your brand meaningful, using news, trends and key moments to get you in front of the right people at the right time.

  2. 02

    Media relations

    Nurturing relationships with editors and publications across luxury travel and lifestyle, opening doors for features, review stays and awards consideration.

  3. 03

    Brand partnerships

    Bringing together likeminded brands across wellness, beauty, fashion, fitness, food and drink, creating partnerships that introduce you to new audiences.

  4. 04

    Influencer collaborations

    Handpicking creators, tastemakers and HNWIs who align with your brand, so every collaboration feels authentic and creates visuals worth sharing.

  5. 05

    Copywriting & storytelling

    Understanding your story and crafting the press materials around it, from releases to media kits, so your key messaging reads with clarity and stays consistent.

  6. 06

    Identity & positioning

    Staying close to the trends and conversations shaping your industry to guide your positioning, your website and your social presence.

The approach

A boutique consultancy means you work with me directly, from the first strategy call to the final press cutting.
